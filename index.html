Fundamental Tools

Output

Tap a pad for the normal sound, long-press for the alt sound. Space plays and stops.

How to use

click or tap anywhere to close ×

PADS

Tap / click
Pad on / off with the normal sound
Long-press
Pad on / off with the alt sound (Shift-click with a mouse)
Drag
Paint the same state across pads
Option-click
Clear a pad (Alt-click on Windows)
Alt pads
Lighter, with a letter: O open hat, B ride bell, X side stick, > accent

SOUND

Audio
Built-in drum sounds through your device's speakers or headphones. Change the output in your device's sound settings.
No sound?
Turn the volume up, and on iPad or iPhone check that Silent mode is off.
MIDI
Plays a MIDI device instead (General MIDI drums, channel 10). Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.
Export MIDI
Saves the whole pattern as a .mid file with tempo and time signature, ready for GarageBand or any DAW.

PAGES

Page boxes
Top-left of the grid: choose the page to see and edit
Green bar
Marks the page that is playing
Pages 1-4
How many pages the pattern plays through
Follow
On: the grid shows the playing page. Off: stay on one page while the rest plays.
Copy / Paste
Duplicate the page you're viewing onto another
Page 1 to all
Copy page 1 onto pages 2-4
Clear
Clear page empties this page; Clear all empties every page

PLAYBACK

Spacebar
Play / stop (with a keyboard)
1 pad
One 16th note. BPM counts quarter notes.
Time sig
Sets steps per page and the numbered beats
